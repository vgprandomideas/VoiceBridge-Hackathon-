Your conversation

Messages are translated into your selected language when translation is available. Original wording is retained for reference. Voice recordings remain in their original language.

If speech is silent, tap Test speaker first. On the receiving phone, tap Turn on spoken messages once to enable automatic speech. Every received message also has Play message for manual replay. Original plays the sender’s wording. Available voices depend on your device.
